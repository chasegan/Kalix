- Performance! Kalix is now ~13% faster for rainfall-runoff models, and about ~5% faster for larger models.
- **Breaking:** The mass balance report now measures the difference between water entering and exiting node (on links). A gauge at the end of a system used to have a 0 mass balance entry, but now reports like a sink with a -ve mass balance entry. See [kalix simulate](https://kalix.org/docs/cli/#kalix-simulate). Mass balance report cards saved by 0.4.10 or earlier can differ from a new one with no change in the simulation. Export your mass balnce reports again with this version.
- Stronger position adopted re negative expressions for `inflow`, `expected_inflow` and `force_flow`. The corresponding recorder will record any negative values, but the model replaces any negatives with 0 internally. See [inflow](https://kalix.org/docs/inflow/) and [gauge](https://kalix.org/docs/gauge/).
