- New! Soil moisture store outputs on the [`sacramento` node](https://kalix.org/docs/sacramento/): `uztw`, `uzfw`, `lztw`, `lzfp` and `lzfs` record the contents of the five stores at the end of each step.
    - Each is a depth in mm over the pervious area, and each fills to its capacity parameter; see [Soil moisture store outputs](https://kalix.org/docs/sacramento-technical/#soil-moisture-store-outputs).
- New! Sacramento calibration can search the ratios `lzpkonlzsk` (`lzpk / lzsk`) and `sarvaonpctim` (`sarva / pctim`), and the IDE's default configuration now does, in place of `lzpk` and `sarva`. See [optimisable parameters](https://kalix.org/docs/optimisable-parameters/#the-ratios-lzpkonlzsk-and-sarvaonpctim).
    - With an upper bound of 1 the ratios keep `lzpk` at or below `lzsk` and `sarva` at or below `pctim`.
    - Existing configurations that map `lzpk` and `sarva` run unchanged.
    - Mapping `pctim` or `lzsk` without its ratio holds `sarva` or `lzpk` where the model file has it. Fors held the ratio instead; the docs show how to do the same.
    - The command line and the Python package list `lzpk` and `sarva`, which the ratios set, in their own block apart from the mapped parameters. The IDE does not show them yet.
    - A ratio mapped without its denominator needs a non-zero denominator in the model file; otherwise the optimisation stops before it starts, since the numerator would be 0 for every candidate.
- Improved: a wrong address in `[parameters]` now stops an optimisation before it starts, where before every candidate failed.
- New! `Select` in the schematic map's context menu grows the selection from the selected nodes and links: all, upstream, downstream, or connected.
    - `Connected downstream` and `Connected upstream` take everything joined to the selection without passing through the river above or below it, so in a braided network they also take the branches that leave or join that stretch of river.
    - With nothing selected, the menu shows `Select all`.
