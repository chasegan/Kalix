- New! `Select` in the schematic map's context menu grows the selection along the network from the selected nodes and links: connected nodes, all nodes, upstream, or downstream.
    - `Upstream including distributaries` and `Downstream including tributaries` also take the branches that leave or join that stretch of river.
    - With no nodes selected, the menu shows `Select all`.
