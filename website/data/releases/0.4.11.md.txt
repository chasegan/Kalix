- Performance! Kalix is now ~13% faster for rainfall-runoff models, and about ~5% faster for larger models.
- The mass balance report now counts the water each node's links carried. Water at an outlet with no link shows at that node, so a gauge at the end of a system reads minus its inflow. See [kalix simulate](https://kalix.org/docs/cli/#kalix-simulate).
    - **Breaking:** a report saved by 0.4.10 can differ from a new one with no change in the simulation. Save your reports again with this version.
- Stronger position adopted re negative expressions for: `inflow`, `expected_inflow` or `force_flow`. The corresponding recorder will record any negative values, but the model replaces them with 0 internally. See [inflow](https://kalix.org/docs/inflow/) and [gauge](https://kalix.org/docs/gauge/).
