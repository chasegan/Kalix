- New! Accounts can have a `debit_factor`, so a user's take of 1 ML debits more (or less) than 1 ML. See [debit factor](https://kalix.org/docs/accounts/#debit-factor).
- New! A storage can order to a `target_volume` as an alternative to `target_level`, and `forecast_volume_adjustment` lets you allow for water you expect to arrive or leave. See [storage](https://kalix.org/docs/storage/).
- New! `[var.*]` blocks can run in the order phase with `phase = order`. See [vars](https://kalix.org/docs/vars/).
- New! Gzipped CSV files (`.csv.gz`) work wherever `.csv.zip` does. See [compressed CSV files](https://kalix.org/docs/input-data/#compressed-csv-files-csvzip-csvgz).
- [`Field` node](https://kalix.org/docs/field/) now follows FAO-56 throughout. See [provenance](https://kalix.org/docs/field/#provenance).
    - Per FAO-56, crop roots now grow from `initial_root_depth` to `root_depth` over the season.
    - Per FAO-56 (Table 22), the depletion fraction `p` is adjusted each day for the crop's evapotranspiration. There is no switch to turn this off.
    - Crop transpiration is now based on their basal coefficient `kcb` rather than `kc`, and a field needs `tew` and `rew` for its surface evaporation layer. Transpiration and soil evaporation are computed separately. 
    - To convert a Kc table, see [coming from a single coefficient](https://kalix.org/docs/field/#coming-from-a-single-coefficient).
    - Storm runoff uses the field's `curve_number`, and the day's curve now follows the dryness of the surface layer (ASCE Manual 70). 
    - `interception` now defaults to 0 (was 0.2).
    - The IDE's field template is a cotton crop on a Vertosol, with parameters from the published literature for the northern Murray–Darling Basin.
- Minor KalixIDE things:
    - `Gzip` and `Gunzip` in the file tree.
    - `Zip` is more robust, and `Duplicate` keeps the whole extension (`flows (1).csv.zip`).
    - Alt+letter opens the menus on Windows and Linux.
    - Shift+scroll pans, and trackpad zoom is smooth.
    - Ctrl+0 (Cmd+0 on macOS) zooms the map to fit.
    - `View → Scroll Pans Map` makes scrolling pan the map.
