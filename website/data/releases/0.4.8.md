- Fixed: daily data stamped at a time other than midnight (e.g. 9am) now keeps its time in output files, e.g. `2020-01-01T09:00:00`. This applies to model outputs and to CSV and Pixie exports from the IDE.
- Changed: [all inputs must share the same time of day](https://kalix.org/docs/input-data/). Daily data at 9am can no longer be mixed with daily data at midnight; Kalix now stops with an error instead of silently shifting one of them by a day.
    - `start` and `end` must also fall on the data's timesteps, e.g. `start = 2020-01-02T09:00:00` for 9am data.
