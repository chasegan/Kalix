  cold-path clarity (`manifestos/performance.md`).

  cross-language fixtures incl. a NaN-payload one), `EngineNames.java` ↔
  `misc_functions.rs::sanitize_name`. Change both sides or neither.

  convert exactly once at the boundary (documented in the STDIO spec + CLAUDE.md).
- The review report's findings go stale as work lands — always re-verify a
  finding against current code before acting on or citing it.

  dev runs; reads never write; typed keys in `PreferenceKeys` are the only
