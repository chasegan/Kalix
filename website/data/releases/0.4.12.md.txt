- An account table refuses `nan` as an initial balance; it used to load and give the account a NaN balance for the whole run. A `nan` size is now reported as not a number rather than as a negative size. See [accounts](https://kalix.org/docs/accounts/).
- A `debit_factor` must now be at least about 2.2e-308. Smaller (subnormal) factors used to load.
