- New! Link nodes using a mouse on the map! Drag from the perimeter of a node and drop on the intended downstream node. The link uses the first free outlet, or re-points the last allowed outlet if all are already used.
- New rainfall-runoff nodes: [AWBM](https://kalix.org/docs/awbm/) (`type = awbm`) and [SURM](https://kalix.org/docs/surm/) (`type = surm`).
    - AWBM is the Australian Water Balance Model with its 8 standard parameters. 
    - `variant = two_tap` selects Hydro Tasmania's AWBM Two Tap version (__BETA!__).
    - SURM is the Simple Urban Runoff Model used by MUSIC and Source.
- New [field](https://kalix.org/docs/field/) node (`type = field`) is Kalix's crop model (__BETA!__).
    - Runs a FAO-56 daily root-zone depletion model with a crop coefficient and a stress coefficient.
    - Field nodes order water to meet soil moisture deficit. For testing purposes currently this is a custom `order` expression. Useful recorders include `this.depletion[-1, 0]` and `this.orders_en_route[-1, 0]`.
    - Surplus inflows are ejected to `ds_1`. Field nodes, unlike any other node, do not support ordering on their ds_1. This is to avoid orders entering from the river return pathway.
- Ordering refinements:
    - You can now ORDER TO `unregulated_user` and THROUGH `regulated_user` nodes by connecting their `ds_2`, `ds_3`, or `ds_4` links. This way a user can supply other nodes such as [field](https://kalix.org/docs/field/) nodes through its pump and accounts.
    - Below a confluence, the travel time comes from the named `regulated` pathway(s) only (longest of these as previously documented).
    - Confluence with neither `regulated` nor `harmony_fraction` defined now sends no orders upstream. This is to force modeller's deliberate intention.
    - Regulated user negative orders now -> 0. Previously, for users with accounts, negative orders slipped through.
    - Confluence `harmony_fraction` recorder showed stale value; now fixed.
    - New storage recorders: `order` and `orders_en_route`. These show the order a storage places to reach its `target_level` (or passes on with `order_through`), and the water it thinks is currently on its way.
    - Splitter nodes now deliver regulated orders down the effluent. The splitter remembers the order and sends it when the water arrives.
    - Splitter nodes now increase orders automatically (like loss nodes) if needed to cover losses to the effluent path.
    - Splitter `table` is now optional. A splitter with no table diverts just what was ordered, representing a regulated offtake such as the head of an irrigation channel.
- Other small things:
    - BETA. `acc.<account>.allocation_pct` for each account, and `acc.<group>.allocation_pct` for the group as a whole (total allocation over total size) reports allocation as a percentage of account size. Note this is the allocation actually held, not the percentage an `allocate` action announced (`ras.<name>.pct`).
    - Huge Pixie files (`.pxt`) can now lazy-load in the Run Manager to avoid consuming memory.
    - New "Show checked" and "Show selected" commands in the Run Manager timeseries tree.
    - Menu items that cannot apply are hidden or greyed, such as "Show checked" with nothing ticked.
    - Minor UX fixes in the Run Manager.
    - The IDE file IO now understands proleptic year 0000.
    - Improvements in editor tab right-click menu behaviour.
